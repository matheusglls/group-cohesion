MetricBrief definition
Mean IFDMean pairwise distance between all fish. For each pair: c = √(a² + b²), where a is the horizontal component and b is the vertical component.
IFD SDStandard deviation of all pairwise IFD values (c): SD = √[Σ(c − mean IFD)² / m], where m is the number of fish pairs.
Mean NNDMean nearest-neighbor distance: for each fish, the distance to its closest fish is found, then these distances are averaged.
Mean FNDMean furthest-neighbor distance: for each fish, the distance to its farthest fish is found, then these distances are averaged.
Hull areaArea of the convex polygon enclosing the outermost fish.
Hull perimeterPerimeter of the convex polygon enclosing the outermost fish.
Mean heightMean perpendicular distance of the fish from the calibrated tank bottom.
Height SDStandard deviation of the perpendicular distance of the fish from the calibrated tank bottom.
Mean relative heightMean height of the fish expressed as a percentage of the calibrated tank height.
Relative height SDStandard deviation of the height of the fish expressed as a percentage of the calibrated tank height.
Mean horizontal IFDMean absolute horizontal component (a) of all pairwise fish distances, measured parallel to the tank bottom.
Horizontal IFD SDStandard deviation of the horizontal components (a) of all pairwise fish distances.
Mean vertical IFDMean absolute vertical component (b) of all pairwise fish distances, measured perpendicular to the tank bottom.
Vertical IFD SDStandard deviation of the vertical components (b) of all pairwise fish distances.
Individual heightPerpendicular distance of each fish from the calibrated tank bottom.
Individual relative heightHeight of each fish expressed as a percentage of the calibrated tank height.

Use the same anatomical reference for every fish. Press Z to undo the last point and Shift+A to clear all fish points.
